Inside a ChatGPT

A three-part series on how a language model works, for people who are starting out and don't have a math background. Every example and number comes from pocketchat, a 30M-parameter GPT I built and trained from scratch on a laptop GPU.

  1. Part 0 — The math of AI, without fearThe five operations behind a language model, measured on a small GPT trained from scratch: vectors, dot products, matrices, softmax and gradient descent.
  2. Part 1 — Inside the modelTokens, attention, blocks and training, following one sentence through a 30M-parameter GPT trained on a laptop.
  3. Part 2 — Teaching the model to chatChat fine-tuning on a small GPT: special tokens, loss masking, what it teaches, and the forgetting it causes.
Written by Andrezza Medeiros Souza. Every number in this series was measured on pocketchat, a small GPT trained from scratch on a laptop. Code: github.com/dedezza1D/pocketchat. To chat with the model and reproduce these numbers on your computer: Try it.
